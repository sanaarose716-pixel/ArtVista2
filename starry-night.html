The Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh

The Starry Night is one of the most recognised paintings in modern art. Its swirling night sky, glowing stars and peaceful village create an expressive view of the world at night.

Artist: Vincent van Gogh

Year: 1889

Medium: Oil on canvas

Location: Museum of Modern Art, New York
