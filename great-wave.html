The Great Wave off Kanagawa

Katsushika Hokusai

This iconic Japanese woodblock print shows enormous waves rising above boats, with Mount Fuji visible in the distance. Its dramatic composition and striking blue tones have influenced artists around the world.

Artist: Katsushika Hokusai

Year: Around 1831

Medium: Colour woodblock print

Series: Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji
