The Kiss

Gustav Klimt

The Kiss is famous for its decorative patterns, golden colours and portrayal of two figures embracing. Gustav Klimt combined symbolic imagery with detailed ornamentation to create this celebrated artwork.

Artist: Gustav Klimt

Year: 1907–1908

Medium: Oil and gold leaf on canvas

Location: Belvedere, Vienna
