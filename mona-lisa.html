Mona Lisa

Leonardo da Vinci

The Mona Lisa is one of the most famous paintings in the world. It is admired for the subject's mysterious smile, the careful use of light and shadow, and the beautiful landscape in the background.

Artist: Leonardo da Vinci

Year: Around 1503–1519

Medium: Oil on poplar wood

Location: Louvre Museum, Paris
