Water Lilies

Claude Monet

Water Lilies is the name of a series of paintings inspired by the water garden at Monet's home in Giverny, France. The paintings explore reflections, colour and changing light on the surface of the pond.

Artist: Claude Monet

Period: 1890s–1920s

Medium: Oil on canvas

Style: Impressionism
